<h2>Minibio</h2>
<p>Dr. Fernando Koch is Director, Forward Research at TELUS Digital and an Industry Professor at Florida Atlantic University, where he directs the Generative Intelligence Lab. Koch studies how people and AI agents make decisions together and how data from intelligent devices can support that collaboration. He explores how people can understand and challenge the decisions of AI systems that keep learning after deployment. His work in Agentic-Oriented Solution Engineering focuses on practical tools that help CTOs build and govern those systems. He teaches generative and agentic AI and works with researchers, startups, and industry partners to put new ideas to the test. He has held senior roles at IBM and Samsung Research and advises startup boards. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science at Utrecht University, has published 100+ scientific papers, has co-edited six books, and holds 90+ patents.</p>
