<h2>Minibio</h2>
<p>Dr. Fernando Koch is Director, Forward Research at TELUS Digital and an Industry Professor at Florida Atlantic University, where he directs the Generative Intelligence Lab. He studies how people and AI agents can make decisions together and how organizations can build trust in systems that keep learning after deployment. His work in Agentic-Oriented Solution Engineering explores how to measure changes in a system’s behavior and determine when people need to intervene. He teaches generative and agentic AI and works with researchers, startups, and industry partners to put new ideas to the test. He has held senior roles at IBM and Samsung Research and advises startup boards. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science at Utrecht University, has published 100+ scientific papers, has co-edited six books, and holds 90+ patents.</p>
